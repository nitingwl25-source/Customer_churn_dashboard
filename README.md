# Telecom Customer Churn Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analysing customer churn in the telecommunications industry using Power BI. The objective is to understand customer retention, identify churn patterns, and analyse the factors influencing customer churn.

An interactive dashboard was developed to provide business insights into customer behaviour, monthly charges, contract types, and service subscriptions.

## Key Analysis & Insights

### 1. Customer Retention Analysis
- Analysed retained and churned customers.
- Evaluated overall customer retention percentage.
- Identified customer churn patterns.

### 2. Churn by Tenure
- Segmented customers into tenure groups:
  - 0–6 months
  - 6–12 months
  - 12–24 months
  - 24+ months
- Observed the highest churn rate among customers with 0–6 months of tenure (52.94%).

### 3. Contract Analysis
- Analysed customer distribution across:
  - Month-to-Month
  - One Year
  - Two Year
- Examined the relationship between contract types and customer retention.

### 4. Service Subscription Analysis
- Analysed monthly charges based on Online Security and Online Backup subscriptions.
- Compared customer spending across service categories.

### 5. Customer Segmentation
- Compared monthly charges between old and new customers.
- Analysed average monthly charges of retained and churned customers by gender.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Data Visualization
- Business Intelligence

## Business Recommendations
- Focus on improving retention among customers in their first six months.
- Investigate reasons behind higher churn among month-to-month contract customers.
- Explore opportunities to increase adoption of additional services.
- Develop targeted customer retention strategies.
